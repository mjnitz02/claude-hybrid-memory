# GATES — irreversible harm

**Violating one of these damages or exposes something that cannot be put back.** They are here,
not in the territory that owns them, because a session can reach the dangerous thing from a repo
whose territory it never loaded — and it cannot know to look for a rule it has not seen.
Correctness and efficiency facts are *not* gates: those are cluster contracts, inlined into the
consuming territory's `MEMORY.md`. **Capped at five.**

The test is **irreversibility, not importance.** Writing to a production box, committing live
credentials, overwriting the only copy of something, publishing something private. If the cost of
getting it wrong is wasted time rather than lost or exposed data, it belongs to its cluster.
